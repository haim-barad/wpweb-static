      What the audit checks

      The six signals that decide whether AI assistants recommend you — or your competitor.

      Schema.org structured data

      Machine-readable entity markup AI models use to recognize your business, products, and FAQs.

      AI crawler access

      Whether GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot are allowed in your robots.txt — blocked sites are invisible in AI answers.

      llms.txt manifest

      The emerging standard that gives AI crawlers a curated map of your most important content.

      Meta & social signals

      Title tags, descriptions, canonicals, and Open Graph fields — quoted verbatim by AI summaries.

      Content citability

      Heading structure, word count, and quotable formatting — the passages AI actually cites.

      E-E-A-T trust signals

      Authorship, entity linking, and contact infrastructure AI uses to judge who to trust.

      What does the AI visibility audit check?

      Six things AI systems rely on: Schema.org structured data, robots.txt access for AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot), an llms.txt manifest, meta and Open Graph signals, content structure and citability, and E-E-A-T trust signals like authorship and entity linking.

      Is the audit really free?

      Yes. Enter any public website URL and get an instant 0–100 score with specific recommendations. No account, no email required to see your score.

      What is a good AI visibility score?

      75+ is good, 90+ is excellent. Most WordPress sites we audit score below 50 because they lack structured data, block AI crawlers, or have no llms.txt — all fixable. That gap is exactly why we built the GEO Pro plan.

      Does this work for non-WordPress sites?

      Yes — the audit works on any public website. Our managed plans are WordPress-focused, but the recommendations are valid for any stack. Note: content rendered only by JavaScript may not be visible to crawlers, which is itself a finding.
